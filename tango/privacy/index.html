Tango Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Tango is provided by Prism Peak Games. Android package: com.prismpeakgames.tango.

Tango is intended for ages 13 and older. Puzzle progress, preferences, theme unlocks and hint credits are saved on your device. Clearing app data removes these local records.

Optional Play Games sign-in uses Google Play Games and Firebase Authentication. Achievement milestones are stored on this phone and can sync to the signed-in Play Games account. Local puzzle milestones may be submitted to the currently signed-in account; ranked Daily streaks belong to their original account. This does not provide cloud saves for regular puzzle progress. Ranked Daily play stores your account identifier, chosen public nickname, puzzle attempt, timing and scores in Tango’s Firebase project. Your nickname, rank and scores can appear on the leaderboard. Ranked timing includes time outside the app.

Tango uses Google AdMob for banners, optional rewarded ads and ads between completed regular puzzles. Google’s SDK may process device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and ad performance data. Requests disable publisher first-party IDs and request non-personalized, G-rated ads with conservative under-age-of-consent treatment for our 13+ audience. This does not eliminate Google’s processing. Where required, Google’s consent form is shown before ad requests. Privacy options are available in Settings when required.

Rewards are granted only after the ad provider confirms completion. Ads may be unavailable offline. Your device’s advertising settings provide additional controls. We do not sell your puzzle progress. Google’s processing is described at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Optional reminders are off until you enable them. Local reminders are scheduled at 9 AM, 1:30 PM, 5 PM and 9 PM in your device timezone; Android may delay delivery. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a registration token and related installation/network information to deliver optional push messages through Google. The token is cached on this device. Turning reminders off cancels local reminders and displayed notifications, disables messaging auto-initialization and requests deletion of the registration token. Notification permission is separate from ad consent and legal acknowledgement.

To request deletion of your Tango online profile and scores, contact prismpeakgames@gmail.com with the nickname and the Google account used for Tango. We may need to verify ownership. Local progress can be removed using Android’s app-data controls.

Contact: prismpeakgames@gmail.com

Service safeguards and your choices

Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity may process installation, app and device-integrity signals to protect online requests. Tango does not enable Firebase Analytics. Google’s services process information under their own retention and privacy policies and may process it internationally. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Local saves remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. Android app backup is disabled. Online ranked records remain until you request deletion, except information we must retain for legal or security reasons. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request. You can request access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights by contacting us. See Tango online data deletion. Google Play Games achievements and activity must be managed separately through Google.

Notification opt-in is optional. Local reminders use your timezone; the scored Daily follows its UTC date. Delivery times are approximate. Refusing reminders does not prevent gameplay. We do not request your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.
