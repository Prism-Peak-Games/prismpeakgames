Delete your Tango online data

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Tango is provided by Prism Peak Games. Android package: com.prismpeakgames.tango.

To request deletion of your Tango online ranked account and associated data, email prismpeakgames@gmail.com with the subject Tango ranked data deletion.

How to identify your account

In Tango, open Leaderboard → Edit profile → Request ranked data deletion to prepare an email with your player reference. You can also email us directly with your nickname and the Google account used for Tango. Do not send passwords or authentication codes. Preparing an email does not send it or delete data automatically. We verify ownership before processing a request.

What is removed

After verification, Prism Peak Games handles deletion of the associated Firebase Authentication account, private ranked profile, attempt records and helper clock, and public leaderboard entries. Shared daily puzzles are not personal account records. Information required for legal or security reasons may be retained; we will explain any applicable exception when handling your request.

Separate records

Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local progress, hint credits, theme unlocks, settings and local reminders, but does not delete online ranked records. Google Play Games achievements and activity are managed separately through Google. Turning reminders off in Tango cancels its local reminders and requests deletion of its Firebase Messaging registration token. Future sign-in may create a new ranked account.

Contact us using the same email for questions about access, correction or deletion.
