Patches account and data deletion

Prism Peak Games provides this process for deleting your Patches Firebase account and associated ranked data.

Request deletion

In Patches, open Weekly leaderboard → Edit profile → Request ranked data deletion. This prepares an email containing your player reference. Review and send the email to submit the request; opening the email does not submit it automatically.

If you no longer have the app, email prismpeakgames@gmail.com with the subject Patches ranked data deletion. Include your leaderboard nickname and, if available, your player reference. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. We may ask for limited additional information to verify account ownership.

What is deleted

After verifying the request, we delete the Patches Firebase Authentication account, private ranked profile, attempts and submitted solutions, ranked clock record, and public weekly leaderboard entries associated with that account. We reply to confirm completion or explain any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons. A request does not delete your Google account.

Device saves and Google Play Games

Clear Patches app storage in Android settings or uninstall to remove local progress, hints, settings and theme unlocks. This local progress cannot be restored from the ranked account. Google Play Games achievements and activity are managed separately in Google's Play Games settings; deleting those does not delete the Firebase records above. Disabling reminders in Patches cancels local reminders and requests deletion of the messaging token.

If you reconnect to Play Games after deletion, the app may create a new Firebase account. Change the sign-in setting for Patches in Play Games if you do not want automatic sign-in.
