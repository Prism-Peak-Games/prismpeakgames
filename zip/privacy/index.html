Zip Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prism Peak Games handles information when you use Zip, our Android puzzle game (package com.prismpeakgames.zipgame).

Information stored on your device

Zip stores puzzle progress, unfinished attempts, completion times, medals, daily streaks, hint balances, unlocked themes, settings, reminder preferences, ad-frequency counters, and the version of the legal notice you accepted. These records support gameplay and are stored locally. Zip does not provide player accounts, a public leaderboard, chat, or cloud progress synchronization.

Advertising

Zip uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for banners, interstitials, and optional rewarded ads. We configure G-rated, non-personalized ads and apply under-age-of-consent treatment to all ad requests. Publisher first-party IDs are disabled. These settings do not make advertising data-free.

Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or advertising identifiers to provide advertising, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Zip does not upload your puzzle saves to Google Mobile Ads. Where Google's consent platform requires it, you can review advertising choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices. The legal acknowledgement is separate from advertising consent.

Optional reminders and Firebase

Reminders are disabled by default. You can enable them after the in-app explanation or in notification settings; Android also asks for notification permission where required. Local reminders use on-device scheduling. When reminders are enabled, permission is available, and Firebase is configured, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes installation identifiers, push tokens, and related device/network information to deliver messages. Zip does not add Firebase Analytics.

Turning reminders off in Zip cancels local reminders, disables messaging auto-initialization, and requests deletion of the push token. Provider-side deletion depends on connectivity and processing. Revoking Android notification permission prevents notifications from being displayed; it is not a request to erase every identifier previously held by Google. Notification routing and recent message IDs are stored locally to open the right screen and avoid duplicate taps.

Support and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you supply and use it to respond. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information. If you choose to copy or share game results, you decide where to send them. Debug builds offer developer notification tests and explicit token copying; these are not normal release features.

Google and Firebase process information needed for the services above under their own policies. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users or our rights, or in a business transfer with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your personal information ourselves.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game data remains until you clear Zip's app storage or uninstall the app. Android app backup is disabled in the current build. Clearing storage or uninstalling also removes locally earned hints and theme unlocks; there is no cloud account from which to restore them. Game reset controls may reset only the progress identified by that control, rather than all app data.

Google retains service data according to its own policies. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to answer requests, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Email prismpeakgames@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights available where you live. We may need limited information to identify your request and will explain any retention required by law. Zip has no player account to delete.

You can manage sound, vibration, and reminders in the game, and advertising identifiers and notification permissions in Android settings. Advertising choices may affect ad availability, including optional rewards.

Security and international processing

Our providers use encrypted network connections for their services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google and other providers may process information outside your country under applicable safeguards.

Children's privacy

Zip is intended for players aged 13 and older, consistent with our other games. We do not ask for contact details, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and handle deletion where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We update the effective date when this policy changes. The game asks you to acknowledge the current legal version. Contact: prismpeakgames@gmail.com.
