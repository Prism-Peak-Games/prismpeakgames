Little Queens Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prism Peak Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Little Queens (the "Game").

Information we handle

Information stored on your device. The Game stores your puzzle progress, scores, earned items, generated leaderboard name and chosen avatar, settings, achievements, and unfinished-game state on your device. This information supports gameplay and preferences.

Leaderboard and Firebase information. On supported mobile platforms, the Game creates an anonymous Firebase identifier. It sends that identifier, your generated leaderboard name, total Adventure score, levels solved, average score, and update time to Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore so that the shared leaderboard works. Your generated leaderboard name and score statistics may be visible to other players. Firebase may also process IP addresses, user-agent information, and service identifiers to provide, secure, and prevent abuse of its services.

Analytics and notifications. On Android, Google Firebase Analytics may process app-instance identifiers, app interactions, approximate region derived from IP address, and device/app information to measure use and notification campaigns. Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a Firebase installation identifier and push-notification token and subscribes the app to a notification topic at startup, even if you do not grant Android notification permission. Android asks for permission before showing notifications where required; you can turn notifications off in Android system settings.

Advertising information. The Android version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent platform. We configure ads as non-personalized and apply under-age-of-consent treatment to all ad requests. Even with those restrictions, Google may collect or share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions such as launches, taps and ad views, diagnostic/performance information, and device or advertising identifiers. These are used to provide ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. The Game does not send your puzzle-board contents or chosen leaderboard name to Google Mobile Ads.

Platform services. If Google Play Games or Apple Game Center connects, the platform provider may process your platform account identifier and achievement activity to authenticate you and synchronize achievements. Store providers also process rating requests under their own terms.

We do not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, camera, payment-card information, or a manually created Little Queens account.

How we use information

  • Operate and save the Game.
  • Provide leaderboards, achievements, ads, notifications, and rewards.
  • Understand app performance and feature use.
  • Keep services secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Comply with law and respond to privacy enquiries.

When information is shared

We share information only as needed with service providers that operate Game features, including Google Firebase, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Mobile Ads, Google Play Games, Google Play, and, on Apple devices, Apple Game Center. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or our rights, or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell your personal information ourselves.

Your choices

You can control sound and vibration in Settings. Where required, Settings shows Privacy choices for advertising preferences. You can manage notification permission, advertising identifiers, ad-personalization choices, Google Play Games, and Game Center through the Game's consent prompt or your device and platform settings. Clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it removes locally stored Game data, subject to device backups controlled by your platform.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall it. Leaderboard records and the anonymous Firebase account are kept while the leaderboard operates, unless deletion is requested or retention is required for security, fraud prevention, or law. Service providers retain information according to their own policies.

You can delete your leaderboard record and anonymous Firebase account from the in-game Privacy Policy screen. This does not erase local puzzle progress. To ask about other information, email prismpeakgames@gmail.com and include your request ID if you have one. A display name alone does not reliably identify your anonymous record.

Security and international processing

We limit the information sent by the Game and use access controls and encrypted network connections supported by our service providers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Our providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to their legal safeguards.

Children's privacy

Little Queens is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not ask players to provide contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and publish the revised policy at this public URL.

Contact and third-party policies

Privacy enquiries: prismpeakgames@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Use.
